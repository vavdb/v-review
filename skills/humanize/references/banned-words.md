# Banned words and phrases

Three tiers. Tier 1 is absolute. Tiers 2 and 3 yield to a writer's voice sample, never to "it sounded fine".

## Tier 1: never, whatever the voice sample says

`delve` · `tapestry` · `crucial` · `furthermore` · `shifting landscape` · `it is worth noting` / `it's worth noting`

## Tier 2: cut on sight unless the sample uses them

**AI vocabulary.** actually (as opener), additionally, albeit, align with, beacon, certainly, comprehensive, cultivate, cutting-edge, elevate, embark, emphasizing, empower, enduring, enhance, essentially, ever-evolving, facilitate, foster, game changer, garner, harness, holistic, interplay, intricate, journey (figurative), key (adjective), landscape (abstract), leverage (verb), meticulous, moreover, multifaceted, navigate (figurative), nuanced, paradigm, paramount, pivotal, quietly, realm, robust, seamless, showcase, streamline, supercharge, synergy, testament, transformative, typically, underscore (verb), unlock, utilize, valuable, various (as vague pluraliser), vibrant, whilst

**Sales language.** boasts a, breathtaking, commitment to, exemplifies, groundbreaking, in the heart of, must-visit, natural beauty, nestled, profound, renowned, rich (figurative), stunning, world-class, best-in-class, next-gen, future-proof, revolutionary, disruptive

**Marketing clichés.** actionable insights, at scale (no number), best practices (no citation), customer-centric, data-driven (as filler), digital transformation, growth hacking, low-hanging fruit, move the needle, quick wins, thought leader, value-add, win-win

**Corporate / Slack / email filler.** bandwidth, circle back, deep dive, double-click on, going forward, loop in, on my radar, per my last email, sync up, touch base, unpack

**Filler openers and phrases.** at the end of the day, at its core, at this point in time, below is / below:, due to the fact that, here's a breakdown, in order to, in summary, in terms of, in the age of, in the ever-evolving landscape, in the world of, in this article, in today's [noun], it goes without saying, it's important to note, let's dive in, overall (as qualifier), that way you can, the reality is, the truth is, when it comes to, with regard to

**Fake-candour and hype phrases.** and honestly?, brutal clarity, blunt honesty, can I be real, here's the kicker, here's the thing, here's what nobody tells you, I'll be honest, I'll be real, let that sink in, let me be clear, lost the plot, painfully clear, plot twist:, read that again, real talk, the uncomfortable truth is, think about it:, this changes everything, this is huge, what if I told you, will never be the same

## Tier 3: often empty adverbs and qualifiers

Cut when they add nothing; keep when they carry real emphasis, contrast, or the writer's spoken rhythm.

just, literally, honestly, simply, actually, truly, really, basically, fundamentally, importantly, crucially, inherently, inevitably, incredibly, significantly, absolutely, very, quite, rather, somewhat, arguably, perhaps, to some extent, in some cases, could potentially, might arguably

## Conviction check (hard rule 5)

Flag any of these unless followed by a choice: "on the other hand", "that said", "some may argue", "it depends", "there are pros and cons", "both sides have a point", "it's a balance", "your mileage may vary". A paragraph that states a claim and then its opposite without picking one is a hard-rule-5 violation.
